About Drone Tag

Drone Tag is the live tracking platform of Abhyuday Bharat Project Pvt. Ltd. for agricultural drones. Each drone carries a tag with a unique Tracker ID. The tag sends the drone's position and photos of the field to this server, and they appear here within seconds.

The photos also reach the farmer: in the Abhyuday Bharat app, the farmer's completed report shows the images sent by the drone of the pilot who did the job.

What you can do

  • See every drone at its latest position on a live map (street map or satellite).
  • Filter drones by application, autonomy and range.
  • Open one drone to see its full flight path, its details and all its photos.
  • Export a drone's positions as a CSV file and its photos as a ZIP file.
  • All times are shown in Indian Standard Time (IST).

How it works

Drone tags send data to POST /api/data (positions and photos), POST /api/telemetry and POST /api/config. Registered users log in with their mobile number to use this dashboard.
