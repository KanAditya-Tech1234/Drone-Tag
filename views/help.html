Help

How do I register myself and my drone?

Open Register. Step 1: your name, email, mobile number and a password. Step 2: your drone's Tracker ID, UIN, category, application, autonomy and range. You are logged in straight away.

What is the Tracker ID?

The ID your drone tag sends with every position and photo (for example DRONE-01). Enter exactly the same ID when you register. If you fly drones for the Abhyuday Bharat app, use the drone tag number you registered there.

How do I see where a drone is?

The Live map shows every drone at its latest position. Click a pin to see its Tracker ID and the time (IST), then "Open drone dashboard".

How do I see a drone's flight path and photos?

Open the Drone dashboard, enter the Tracker ID and press "Show drone". The map shows the latest flight; pick an earlier one under "Flight". Scroll down for all its photos; click one to enlarge it. The page refreshes by itself every 5 seconds.

What do the map markers mean?

The green pin is where the flight started. While the drone tag is on and sending, the arrow shows its current position and points in its direction of travel. When the tag stops sending (for example it is switched off), the flight is marked as ended after about 2 minutes of silence and a red pin appears at the last position received. The legend in the bottom-left corner of the map shows the same.

How do I filter drones?

On the Live map, choose an application, autonomy or range and press Apply. These come from drone registrations, so drones nobody has registered only show when the filters are set to "All".

How do I export data?

On the Drone dashboard, "Export CSV" downloads the drone's positions (date and time in IST, latitude, longitude, altitude and photo links). "Export images" downloads all its photos in one ZIP file.

My drone does not appear

Check that the drone tag is on and has mobile signal, and that it sends exactly the registered Tracker ID. The first upload after a quiet period can take up to a minute while the server wakes up.
